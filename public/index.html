Each claim is checked against the closing betting spread since 2015. If the odds already account for it, the claim gives no extra edge, and it is not used in the model.

    Where the percentages come from. Two views of team strength are combined. Sportsbooks: the posted moneyline with the bookmaker's margin removed, or, where no line is posted yet, ratings fitted to this season's point spreads. Results: each team's scoring margin and its efficiency per play (expected points added), both adjusted for the strength of the opponents faced. Blend uses the posted line where there is one and averages the two ratings for later games; in a test on the 2021 to 2025 seasons that mix predicted later games better than either view alone. The buttons under the team menu switch between them. Lines as of .

    Live scores. When the live score feed is connected, a game in progress uses an in-game win chance built from the current margin, the time left and the pregame line, and a finished game counts in the standings.

    Unpicked games are simulated 8,000 times using those percentages with some random variation in team strength. These are estimates, not betting advice.

    Ties in the standings follow the NFL's procedure: head-to-head, division record, common games, conference record, strength of victory and strength of schedule. If teams are still level, scores are simulated for the remaining games and the points steps are applied: combined ranking in points scored and allowed within the conference, then league-wide, then net points. Three rarely reached steps are skipped (net points in common games or conference games, and net touchdowns) before the final coin flip. Injuries and quarterback changes are not modeled beyond what the betting lines reflect, and picks cannot be ties.
