Where the percentages come from. Games marked Sportsbook line use the posted moneyline with the bookmaker's margin removed. Games marked Rating estimate have no line yet, so the win chance comes from team ratings fitted to this season's sportsbook point spreads, weighted toward recent weeks, plus home-field advantage. Lines as of Nov. 16, 2026.

Live scores. When the live score feed is connected, a game in progress uses an in-game win chance built from the current margin, the time left and the pregame line, and a finished game counts in the standings.

Unpicked games are simulated 4,000 times using those percentages with some random variation in team strength. These are estimates, not betting advice.

Ties in the standings are broken by head-to-head, division record, common games and conference record. Later steps such as strength of victory are replaced by a coin flip, so a close race can differ from the official order. Injuries and quarterback changes are not modeled, and picks cannot be ties.
